# Product

## Register
brand

## Company and audience
SeventeenLabs is an independent creative software company, the umbrella brand behind a family of tools for generative media. The audience is filmmakers, independent creators, small studios, and creative teams exploring new production methods.

## Positioning
SeventeenLabs builds creative software for generative media. The first product is a generative video editor that brings generative models directly onto a timeline. The company is not the name of the editor. The product name is undecided; do not invent a final name or describe proposed names as established brands.

## Product status
The editor is in development. The website shows a clearly labeled interactive concept preview, not a working application. Do not claim specific model integrations, pricing, a launch date, or available capabilities without confirmation. Early access is a free mailing list with explicit consent, not a paid plan application.

## Voice
Cinematic, precise, curious. Simple confident language, human creative control, specific filmmaking ideas. Avoid business automation, consulting funnels, unsupported metrics, customer logos, fabricated news, or enterprise governance claims.

## Experience
Visitors should understand the company, discover its first product, learn the approach, and join the early-access list. Home, products, about, early access, and legal pages share a consistent company identity. Existing historical content can remain accessible without leading the navigation.

## Design direction
The user requested a clean rebuild based on https://bfl.ai/. Use cinematic full-bleed imagery, confident large sans-serif type, deep forest-charcoal neutrals, pale lavender CTAs, compact navigation, and generous section spacing. Keep SeventeenLabs' identity distinct from BFL.

## Accessibility
Semantic structure, responsive layouts, clear keyboard focus, accessible labels, reduced-motion support, honest form success and failure states, and no unannounced autoplay.
